### Network Optimization Problem for EC524

#### Problem
In a given network there are nodes and routes. Each node has either a supply of
commodity types 1-4 or has a demand of commodity types 1-4. Between any two nodes, there is a 
maximum capacity of goods that can be shipped at a given time. Further,
there is a cost associated with shipping each commodity along any particular route.

Request: Satisfy the supply and demand of each node and do not violate the max
capacity of any particular route while minimizing the cost to transport goods.
